**The short version.** ETIAS is a travel authorisation that visa-exempt travelers will need before visiting 30 European countries. It will cost EUR 20. It is not running yet, and nobody can apply for it today. Everything below was checked against the official ETIAS website on 17 September 2026.

## What ETIAS is

ETIAS stands for the European Travel Information and Authorisation System. It is an online authorisation for people whose nationality does not need a visa for a short stay. You fill in a form, pay the fee, and get an answer that is linked to your passport.

It is not a visa. The official site says it only lets you enter and stay for a short stay in the countries that require it. It does not promise entry either. Border guards still check every traveler, and they can refuse anyone who does not meet the entry conditions.

## Why you cannot apply yet

ETIAS is not in operation. The official ETIAS website says no applications are being collected, and that the EU will announce the start date several months before launch. Its FAQ adds that no action is required from travelers at this point.

That has a plain consequence. **Anyone taking an ETIAS application or an ETIAS fee from you today is not selling what they claim**, because there is nowhere to send the application. The official site warns that there are many unofficial ETIAS websites, and that some falsely claim to accept applications or charge higher fees.

When ETIAS does start, you will apply on the official ETIAS website or the official ETIAS app. A travel agency or another intermediary will be allowed to apply for you if you authorise it. Anything it charges on top of the EUR 20 fee goes to the intermediary.

## Who will need it

You will need ETIAS if all of these apply to you:

- you are not a national of the EU
- your nationality does not need a visa for a short stay in the countries that require ETIAS
- you do not hold a residence permit issued by one of those countries, other than Cyprus

The EU says ETIAS will apply to nationals of 59 visa-exempt countries and territories, including the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and Japan. UK nationals, including British overseas citizens, British protected persons and British subjects, will need it unless they benefit from the withdrawal agreement between the EU and the UK.

Nationals of the 30 countries that require ETIAS, and nationals of Ireland, do not need it.

If your nationality needs a Schengen visa, ETIAS does not apply to you. You apply for the visa instead.

## The 30 countries

Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czechia, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Italy, Latvia, Liechtenstein, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden and Switzerland.

## What it will cost, and how long it will last

| Question | What the official ETIAS website says |
|---|---|
| Fee | EUR 20, not refundable |
| Who pays nothing | Applicants under 18 or over 70, and family members of EU nationals who meet the conditions |
| How long it is valid | Three years, or until your passport expires if that comes first |
| How long you can stay | Up to 90 days in any 180-day period. Time in Cyprus is counted separately |
| How fast the answer comes | Most applications within minutes, with a reply within 96 hours. Up to 30 days if you are asked for more information or invited to an interview |

## ETIAS is not EES

The Entry/Exit System is a different thing, and it is already running. According to the European Commission, EES has been fully operational since 10 April 2026. It registers non-EU travelers on a short stay each time they cross the external border, whether they need a visa or not, and it has replaced passport stamps.

## What to do today

Nothing needs doing for ETIAS yet. The one thing worth knowing now is whether your passport is visa exempt at all. That decides whether ETIAS will apply to you once it starts, or whether you need a visa instead.
